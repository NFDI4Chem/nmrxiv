# Submit from ELN

An **electronic lab notebook (ELN)** or ELN-connected repository can send your NMR data to nmrXiv for you. You prepare and describe your data where you already work, and nmrXiv publishes it as a citable sample with a DOI. You do not need to upload files by hand.

## How it works

1.  You submit a sample from your ELN or its repository.
2.  nmrXiv receives it, processes the data and publishes it as an individual sample.
3.  You get an email with the identifier and DOI of your sample, or a message explaining why it could not be completed.

Samples that arrive from an ELN are published as **individual samples** ("Publish each sample on its own"), not as a project. See [Publication Deposition](/submission-guides/publication-deposition.html) for the difference.

## Before you start

-   You need a registered [nmrXiv account](/submission-guides/registration-and-account.html). The submission is created under this account and all emails are sent to its address.
-   Your data should include the sample with its molecule (structure) and at least one processed NMR spectrum.
