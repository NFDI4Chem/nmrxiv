# Submission Process

nmrXiv offer a seamless and straightforward submission process, allowing you to submit NMR data easily in just three steps.
This documentation guides you through each step of the submission process.
nmrXiv supports two submission routes. Choose the route that matches why you are sharing the spectra.

This page explains the complete submission flow from upload to publication, and shows where the two routes differ.

## Publication Deposition

Use **Publication Deposition** when the NMR data belongs to a manuscript, preprint, thesis, or another publication-style research output.

Publication Deposition is intended for complete research submissions that should be published as formal, citable records with richer publication metadata.

[Open the Publication Deposition guide](/submission-guides/publication-deposition.html)

## Community Contribution

Use **Community Contribution** when you want to contribute useful reference spectra to the open community without linking them to a manuscript or full publication record.

Community Contribution is intended for standalone, reusable reference spectra that can be shared quickly with the broader community.

[Open the Community Contribution guide](/submission-guides/community-contribution.html)
